Build a high-conversion, scalable affiliate system for a prop trading platform (FundedWealth).

GOAL:
Create an aggressive but profitable affiliate system that drives rapid user acquisition in India while maintaining business margins.

---

🎯 CORE PRINCIPLES

1. Only paid users count as referrals
2. No commission for signups or logins
3. Fraud-proof tracking system
4. High commission for growth (30%–50%)
5. Milestone-based rewards (hard to unlock)
6. Delayed payouts (anti-refund protection)

---

💰 COMMISSION STRUCTURE

Implement dynamic tier-based commissions:

Tier 1 (Starter):

* 1–20 sales
* 30% commission

Tier 2 (Pro):

* 21–100 sales
* 35% commission

Tier 3 (Elite):

* 101–300 sales
* 40% commission

Tier 4 (Apex):

* 300+ sales
* 50% commission

Rules:

* Commission applies ONLY on successful payment
* Commission auto-calculated per transaction
* Store commission history per user

---

🎁 REWARD SYSTEM (GROWTH ENGINE)

Milestone rewards:

* 30 sales → ₹5,000 bonus
* 75 sales → ₹15,000 bonus
* 150 sales → iPhone reward
* 400 sales → MacBook reward
* 800+ sales → Bike reward

Rules:

* Only VALID purchases count
* Refunds cancel reward eligibility
* Rewards locked until verification

---

🧠 REFERRAL TRACKING SYSTEM

Build system with:

* Unique referral link per affiliate
* Cookie + backend tracking
* Track:

  * clicks
  * signups
  * purchases
  * revenue generated

Store per user:

* total clicks
* total conversions
* total earnings
* current tier

---

🛡 FRAUD PROTECTION

Implement:

* Block self-referrals (same IP/device/email)
* Detect multiple accounts from same IP
* Prevent coupon stacking abuse
* Flag suspicious activity

Add:

* "Under Review" status for risky accounts

---

💸 PAYOUT SYSTEM

Rules:

* Minimum payout: ₹1,000
* Payout delay: 5–7 days (holding period)
* Cancel commission if:

  * refund happens
  * fraud detected

Payment methods:

* UPI
* Bank transfer

Affiliate dashboard should include:

* available balance
* pending balance
* withdrawn history

---

📊 AFFILIATE DASHBOARD UI

Design modern dashboard with:

1. Overview cards:

   * Total Earnings
   * Pending Earnings
   * Total Sales
   * Conversion Rate

2. Charts:

   * Daily earnings graph
   * Sales trend

3. Referral tools:

   * Copy referral link button
   * Share to WhatsApp button

4. Rewards progress:

   * Progress bar to next milestone
   * Locked/unlocked rewards

5. Transaction table:

   * Date
   * User
   * Plan purchased
   * Commission earned

---

🔥 MARKETING OPTIMIZATION FEATURES

Add:

* Leaderboard (top affiliates)
* Monthly contests
* Real-time notifications:
  "You earned ₹X from a new referral"

---

📜 TERMS & CONDITIONS (STRICT)

Enforce:

* Only completed purchases count
* No commission for signup/login
* Self-referral prohibited
* Refund = commission reversal
* Company can suspend fraudulent accounts
* Rewards subject to verification

---

⚡ PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

* Fast load dashboard
* Real-time tracking updates
* Secure backend (prevent manipulation)

---

🎯 FINAL OUTPUT

System must:

* Maximize affiliate motivation
* Maintain business profitability
* Be scalable to 20,000+ users
